The Counter

An Albanian-owned counter, not a chain.

Dugagjini Meats is an Albanian family-owned butcher shop that's held its spot on Riopelle Street for years. Regulars mention the oxtail, the center-cut pork chops, and the lamb chops most often — cut in front of you, at prices shoppers consistently call fair for Eastern Market.

The case goes beyond the usual fresh cuts: seafood (jumbo shrimp, king crab legs, snow crab legs, ocean perch, sardines), poultry, dairy, and seasoning sit alongside cured specialties like prosciutto, salami, and ham. Halal meats are available year-round, with the full range especially highlighted around Ramadan. Everything is sold retail or wholesale, with delivery to restaurants and other businesses.

Good to Know

Common questions, answered plainly.

Is the meat halal? Yes — halal meats are available at the counter, with the full range especially highlighted during Ramadan.
Do you sell wholesale? Yes — retail and wholesale, with delivery available to restaurants and other businesses.
Can I get a custom cut? Yes — tell the counter what you need and how you want it cut.
Is it just meat? No — the case also carries seafood (shrimp, crab legs, ocean perch, sardines), dairy, and seasoning, alongside cured deli items like prosciutto and salami.
